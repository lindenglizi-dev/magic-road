## 5. 周期行为与延迟

- Keep the original RAW10 demosaic and AWB path; instantiate axis_gamma_rgb96 between the actual AWB RGB96 output and ball_runtime.
- Gamma applies the same one-clock registered delay to all RGB channels, valid, early and last. Its early remains an advance FIFO notification; the pixel-aligned SOF generated by ball_runtime stays private to the packer.
- From the AWB boundary the gamma-plus-runtime pixel path has eight registered cycles and the independent early path has two. These values describe RTL stages, not measured board timing.
- Camera status is coherent across domains; runtime freezes it at advance frame boundary before OSD. A fourth text row shows FPS/EXP/G/RB/WB/CAL without any extra video register stage.
- Expose board-key long-press toggles without adding any video/early register stage. D5/A9 short requests commit on early after debounced release.
- Gamma1plus runtime7gives8pixel stages after AWB; early still2stages. Original camera/DDR/HDMI ports and clocks preserved.

## 7. 边界、反压与错误

- Reset or configuration change has priority over old results.
- Video invalid cycles must not advance pixel coordinates or packer phase.
- Conveyor V1 requires disjoint X projections, one ball per gate and three clear gate frames.

## 8. CDC 与综合约束

- Synthesizable Verilog-2001; Anlogic PH1P35MDG324/-3.
- W is a multiple of16; projection Y coordinates are10bit, so H<=1023. Default1280x720.
- No video ready/backpressure or frame-dropping substitution; detector faults do not alter video byte count.
- Do not replace the advance I_tuser semantics with pixel-aligned SOF; video_in resets its asynchronous FIFO from the early notification. Camera register and ready wiring remain the baseline contract.

## 9. 验证与验收

- sim/tb_ball_core.v:147-plus frames with split/hold100/dropout2/last column/BCD and byte ordering.
- sim/tb_ball_runtime.v:28 frames with boundary commits and late-result rejection.
- sim/tb_ball_hdmi.v:four complete720P frames and actual DDR capture.
- sim/tb_ball_lowlight.v: instantiate the actual axis_gamma_rgb96 definition from this source and verify gamma, valid, early and last together.
- sim/tb_ball_hdmi_lowlight.v: gamma plus runtime preserves four complete720P packed video frames. Native full-project synthesis/implementation is separate evidence.
- sim/tb_ball_runtime.v checks status frame freeze; sim/tb_ball_hdmi_lowlight.v carries explicit FPS060 EXP0480 G16 RB1 WB1 through actual720P DDR capture.
